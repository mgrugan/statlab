Problem #402

Efficacy of Treatment-X: A Two-Sample T-Test

bolt Medium Hypothesis Testing Clinical Trials
Success Rate 64.2%

Scenario

A pharmaceutical company has conducted a Phase II clinical trial for "Treatment-X" aimed at reducing systolic blood pressure. The study involved two groups: a Treatment Group (n=45) receiving the drug, and a Control Group (n=50) receiving a placebo.

Data Schema: clinical_trial.csv

group "treatment" | "control"
bp_reduction Numeric (mmHg)

Objective

Perform a two-sample Welch's t-test to determine if there is a statistically significant difference in mean blood pressure reduction between the groups. Assume α = 0.05.

  • check_circle Calculate the t-statistic and the p-value.
  • check_circle Determine if the null hypothesis should be rejected.
  • check_circle Return a dictionary/list containing {"t_stat": float, "p_val": float, "reject": bool}.
